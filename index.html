Blender Draft rules

Best of 3. The coin-toss winner is Team A and picks Map 1; Team B picks Map 2; Map 3 is the decider.

  • Team A, Map 1 comp is banned for Team A on Map 2 and Map 3.
  • Team B, Map 1 comp is banned for Team B on Map 2 only.
  • Team B, Map 2 comp is banned for Team B on Map 3.
  • Bans only apply to the team that played the comp. The other team can still pick those agents.
